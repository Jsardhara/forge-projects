# Monetization Plan — AITokenProxy

## The Pitch

"Uber capped AI spending at $1,500/mo. AITokenProxy cuts your AI token bill by 50-80% — so you never need a cap."

## Revenue Model

### Tier 1: Free (Developer Acquisition)
- CLI tool + local proxy: free, open source
- 10,000 compressions/month
- Community support
- **Goal**: Get developers hooked on the savings

### Tier 2: Pro — $29/mo per seat
- Unlimited compressions
- Web dashboard with team analytics
- Priority compression algorithms
- Email support
- **Target**: Individual developers spending $100+/mo on AI APIs

### Tier 3: Team — $79/mo per seat
- Everything in Pro
- Team management + RBAC
- Shared compression profiles
- Slack/Discord alerts
- Audit logs
- **Target**: Engineering teams of 5-50

### Tier 4: Enterprise — Custom pricing
- Self-hosted option
- SSO/SAML
- Custom compression models
- SLA
- Dedicated support
- **Target**: Companies spending $10k+/mo on AI

## Market Sizing

- **TAM**: 50M+ developers worldwide use AI coding tools
- **SAM**: 5M developers spending $50+/mo on AI APIs
- **SOM**: 50,000 teams in Year 1 (0.01% of TAM)
- **Revenue potential**: 50k teams × 5 seats × $79/mo = $19.75M/month

## Go-to-Market

1. **Launch day**: "I saved $2,400/mo on AI tokens with this proxy" — Product Hunt + HN Show HN
2. **Week 1-2**: Blog post series — "How Uber's AI budget crisis creates a $10B market"
3. **Week 3-4**: Partner with AI coding tool communities (Claude Code Discord, Cursor forums)
4. **Month 2**: Freemium funnel — free CLI users get upgrade prompts when they hit limits
5. **Month 3**: Enterprise outreach — target companies with >$50k/mo AI spend

## Competitive Moat

- **Data flywheel**: More users = better compression models = more savings = more users
- **Switching cost**: Once teams integrate the proxy, they won't go back to uncompressed
- **Network effects**: Team features create lock-in
- **First-mover**: Nobody is offering token compression as a service yet

## Unit Economics

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Cost per user (infra) | ~$2/mo |
| Gross margin | 93%+ |

## Key Metrics to Track

- Tokens compressed per user per day
- Savings percentage (target: 50%+)
- Free-to-paid conversion rate (target: 5%)
- Churn rate (target: <5% monthly)
